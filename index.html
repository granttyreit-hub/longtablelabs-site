Why “Cadent”?

ca·dent · /ˈkeɪd(ə)nt/ · adjective

Falling in a regular rhythm — from the Latin cadere, “to fall,” the same root as cadence. A cadence is the steady beat a thing moves to.

Every household has one: practices and pickups, dinners and bedtimes, the week's recurring shape. Cadent Family helps you keep it — together, on everyone's phone.
