Common questions

Where can I get Cadent Family?

On Google Play, for Android phones and tablets. Android today on Google Play; other platforms later.

What does it cost?

Cadent Family membership is $1.99/month on Google Play. One membership covers everyone in the family and includes 10 schedule imports a month; unused imports roll over up to 20. It is billed through Google Play and can be cancelled there at any time.

How does the photo-to-calendar feature work?

You photograph a paper schedule; the app reads the dates, times and repeats it finds and shows them to you as a list. You approve the events you want before any of them are added. Nothing goes on your calendar without you. The image is read using Anthropic's AI API — see the privacy policy for details.

Can I keep some events private?

Yes. Each event can be visible to everyone in your family, to just you, or to a chosen list of people. Parents can see their children's events while keeping their own private from other adults.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email privacy@longtablelabs.com from your account's email address and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm and remove your personal data and the content you own. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets, from Google Play. Other platforms may follow; we only claim a platform once you can actually install it there.

Echo Night

Where can I get Echo Night?

It is in testing on Google Play and not yet open to everyone. The link will appear on the Echo Night page when it is.

Does Echo Night collect anything?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no in-app purchases; your progress is saved only on your device. Between flights it shows short videos for Cadent Family, fetched from our own site, and nothing about you is sent. The Echo Night privacy policy has the detail.

Something in the game is broken

Email support@longtablelabs.com with your phone model, which hunting ground and night you were on, and what happened.

Still stuck?

Email support@longtablelabs.com and tell us what happened — what you were doing, and what you expected. That's the fastest way for us to help.
