Privacy Policy

Echo Night, a game by Longtable Labs LLC

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Echo Night is a single-player game made by Longtable Labs LLC (“we”, “us”). The short version: Echo Night collects no information about you. This page says the same thing in the detail that app stores ask for. If anything is unclear, write to support@longtablelabs.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Echo Night has:

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
  • No network connection. The game does not contact any server, ours or anyone else’s, and plays fully offline.
  • No advertising. No ad networks, no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not know who plays, how long, or on what device.
  • No in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress — which nights you have completed, your Catch Log and your settings — is saved on your own device so the game can continue where you left off. It never leaves the device, and deleting the game deletes it.

Google Play

When you install or update Echo Night through Google Play, Google processes that transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive no personal information from it.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, there is no personal information from children or anyone else for us to hold, share or delete.

Changes to this policy

If Echo Night ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about Echo Night or this policy: support@longtablelabs.com.
