Privacy Policy

Echo Night, a game by Longtable Labs LLC

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Echo Night is a single-player game made by Longtable Labs LLC (“we”, “us”). The short version: Echo Night collects no information about you. This page says the same thing in the detail that app stores ask for. If anything is unclear, write to support@longtablelabs.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Echo Night has:

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
  • No network use, with one exception. The game contacts no server, ours or anyone else’s, except to download the short videos described below. Play works fully offline.
  • No third-party advertising. No ad networks, no ad SDKs, no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not know who plays, how long, or on what device.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • One permission: vibration, for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Videos for Cadent Family

Between flights, Echo Night shows short promotional videos for Cadent Family, another Longtable Labs app. These videos are downloaded from Longtable’s own website, and nothing about you or your device is sent when they are fetched. They are not served by an ad network, and there are no third-party advertisements.

What stays on your device

Your progress — which nights you have completed, your Catch Log and your settings — is saved in the app’s private storage on your own device so the game can continue where you left off. It never leaves the device, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

Google Play

When you install or update Echo Night through Google Play, Google processes that transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive no personal information from it.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, there is no personal information from children or anyone else for us to hold, share or delete.

Changes to this policy

If Echo Night ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about Echo Night or this policy: support@longtablelabs.com.
