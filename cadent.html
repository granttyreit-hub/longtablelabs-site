What it does — and only what it does

Every point below is built, shipped and working in the app on Google Play today.

📷

Photograph a paper schedule

A school newsletter, a sports card, a party invite. It reads the dates, times and repeats, and shows you what it found before anything is added.

✓

Nothing added without you

Every event it suggests is reviewed first. You keep the ones you want, drop the rest, and only then is it on everyone’s phone.

🔒

Private when it needs to be

Mark an event for everyone, just you, or a chosen few. Parents see their children’s days; grown-ups keep their own appointments to themselves.

🔗

Share with the people around your family

Give a grandparent, a co-parent in another home or a coach your contact link. Each of you keeps your own calendar and shares only the events you choose.

⭐

Chores that earn rewards

Chores earn points, with streaks and a weekly leaderboard. A parent sets the rewards, kids ask to spend their points, and a parent approves the treat. Earned stays earned.

📱

A kitchen tablet the kids can use

Kid mode locks a shared tablet to one child’s view behind a PIN that stays on that device. They tick their chores and see their day; the rest of the family’s settings stay out of reach.

🌱

Grows up with your kids

Children don’t need accounts; a parent sets everything up. When a child is old enough for their own phone, they claim their place in the family and keep every point and streak they earned.

🔄

Everyone stays in step

Add or change something and it appears on everyone’s phone as it happens. Open the app after a day away and it catches up on its own.

📋

More than a calendar

Shopping lists everyone adds to, reminders and alarms with quiet hours, the school or team calendar followed by link, and themes, fonts and avatars to make it yours. In 13 languages.

How the photo feature works

Photograph a school newsletter, a sports card or a party invite, or choose a picture you already took. The app reads the page into suggested events with dates, times and places filled in. Nothing reaches your calendar until you tick the ones you want.

1

Take a photo

However the schedule arrived — a newsletter, a season card, the sheet on the fridge.

2

It reads the dates

Dates, times, places and repeats, shown to you as a plain list of what it found.

3

You approve

Keep the events you want, drop the rest, and they are on everyone’s phone.

The photo is read by Anthropic’s API and is not used to train their models; if you never use the photo feature, no images are sent. Details in the privacy policy. Watch it happen in the short demos on the home page.

Private by design

Your calendar is visible only to your family and to the people you share an event with, and that is enforced in the app and again in the database. No ads. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. A parent sets up the family; children do not need accounts of their own.
