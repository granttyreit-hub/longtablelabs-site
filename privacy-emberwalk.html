EmberWalk privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

EmberWalk (com.longtablelabs.emberwalk) is made by Longtable Labs LLC. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

What stays on your phone

  • Your routes and GPS points.
  • Your step counts and activity recognition results (walking, driving or cycling), which are used only to check that an outing was on foot.
  • Your journal entries, photos and voice notes.
  • Your streaks, Pebbles and awards.

Voice notes are turned into text by your phone's own on-device speech service. We never receive any of this.

What leaves your phone

If Weather for outings is on (it can be switched off on the Today screen), the app sends your rough area, rounded to about 10 km, to Open-Meteo once per outing. This is used to look up the weather for weather awards. We don't receive or keep it. Open-Meteo's own terms cover its servers.

Permissions

  • Location: to measure your outing while you're tracking one, with a notification shown.
  • Physical activity: to count steps and check the outing was on foot.
  • Microphone: for spoken journal notes.
  • Camera: for journal photos.
  • Notifications: to show the tracking notification.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling EmberWalk deletes everything. We hold no copy.

Children

EmberWalk is not directed at children under 13.
