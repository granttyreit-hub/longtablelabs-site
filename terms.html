What the app does

Cadent Family is a shared calendar, chore and list app for one family. It can read a photo of a paper schedule and propose events from it. The app proposes; you decide. Nothing from a photo reaches your calendar until you approve it.

Proposed events can be wrong: a date, a time or a repeat may be misread. Check each one before you approve it, and do not rely on the app for anything where a missed or wrong time could cause harm, such as medication or legal deadlines.

Your account

You sign in with a Google account or an email address. Keep your sign-in to yourself; you are responsible for what is done with your account. An adult who creates a family manages it: who is in it, who is a parent, and whether it continues.

Children

Children under 13 do not get their own accounts. A parent or guardian adds them to the family, and the adults in the family enter and manage their information. A parent may later give a grown child their own sign-in, as the privacy policy describes. If you add a child, you confirm that you are their parent or guardian, or have their parent's or guardian's permission.

Membership

Cadent Family offers one optional membership: an auto-renewing monthly subscription, sold through Google Play billing. It adds schedule imports for the whole family. The app shows the current price from Google Play, in your currency, before you buy.

  • The subscription renews each month until you cancel it.
  • Cancel it in Google Play. It then runs to the end of the month you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under Google's refund policies.
  • Deleting your account or your family does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.

Payment is processed by Google under its own terms. We never see your card or other payment details.
